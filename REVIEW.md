# Read-Only Review Instructions

All reviews must follow [AI_POLICY.md](AI_POLICY.md).

Review learner-written work without changing it and without supplying a solution. For each finding, state its severity, location, impact, relevant concept, a guiding question or non-solution hint, and a behavior the learner can verify in plain language.

Do not provide corrected code, replacement text, snippets, patches, diffs, tests, commands, pseudocode, or step-by-step implementation instructions. Do not commit, apply, or suggest an automated fix.
